# Chapter 6 — Class I Method for Wing Planform Design and for Sizing and Locating Lateral Control Surfaces

**Source:** J. Roskam, *Airplane Design, Part II: Preliminary Configuration Design and Integration of the Propulsion System*, Chapter 6 "Class I Method for Wing Planform Design and for Sizing and Locating Lateral Control Surfaces," printed pp. 141–166 (PDF indices 152–177).

The chapter gives a step-by-step method for these wing planform characteristics:

1. Size (area), `S`
2. Aspect ratio, `A`
3. Sweep angle, `Λ`
4. Thickness ratio, `t/c`
5. Airfoils
6. Taper ratio, `λ`
7. Incidence angle, `i_w`, and twist angle, `ε_t`
8. Dihedral angle, `Γ_w`
9. Lateral control surface size and layout

Items 1 and 2 come from the preliminary sizing of Part I. Items 3–9 are found here. The
method is Step 6 of preliminary-design sequence I of Chapter 2.

Step 3 of sequence I (Chapter 2, p. 11) already decided whether the configuration is:

1. Conventional (tail aft)
2. Flying wing (no horizontal tail or canard)
3. Tandem wing
4. Canard
5. Three surface
6. Joined wing

---

## 6.1 A Procedure for Wing Planform Design and for Sizing and Locating Lateral Control Surfaces

### Step 6.1 — Flying wing check
If the airplane is a flying wing, all items of Chapter 4 must be integrated into the
wing. This has a major effect on the wing layout, so look at it first. If the airplane is
not a flying wing, go to Step 6.2.

### Step 6.2 — Overall structural wing configuration
Choose between:

1. Cantilever wing
2. Braced (strutted) wing

Tables 6.1 through 6.12 are the initial guide. Braced wings are used mostly on relatively
low speed airplanes: above about **200 kts** the trade between the profile and
interference drag increments of the struts and the wing weight saving is unfavorable.

### Step 6.3 — Overall wing/fuselage arrangement
Choose between: 1. High wing, 2. Mid wing, 3. Low wing. Tables 6.1 through 6.12 are the
initial guide; also review the configurations of Chapter 3.

Ratings of the wing/fuselage arrangements (p. 142). They are correct **only if
"everything else" is the same**. 1 means preferred, 3 means least preferred:

| Criterion | High wing | Mid Wing | Low Wing |
|---|---|---|---|
| Interference Drag | 2 | 1 | 3 |
| Lateral Stability | 1 | 2 | 3 |
| Visibility from Cabin * | 1 | 2 | 3 |
| Landing Gear Weight | 3 ** | 2 | 1 |

Footnotes as printed:
- `*` strongly dependent on where the wing passes through the fuselage
- `**` if the gear is retracted into the fuselage, gear weight is not necessarily a
  factor. In that case the landing gear often requires a "bump fairing" which causes
  additional drag.
