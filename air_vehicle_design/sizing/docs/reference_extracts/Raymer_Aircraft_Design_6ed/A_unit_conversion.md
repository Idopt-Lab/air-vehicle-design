# Appendix A — Unit Conversion

**Source:** Raymer, *Aircraft Design: A Conceptual Approach*, 6th ed. (AIAA, 2018), Appendix A
"Unit Conversion," printed pp. 959–962.

Two conversion tables covering the same 15 quantity categories (length and range; area; volume;
velocity/climb/Ps; mass and weight; force and weight; pressure; density; weight loading; power;
specific fuel consumption; power SFC; fuel specific energy; range parameter; loiter parameter;
temperature): Table A.1 converts FPS (English) units to MKS (metric), Table A.2 converts MKS back
to FPS. No numbered equations, no figures — pure conversion-factor tables.

---
