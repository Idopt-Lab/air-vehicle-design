# Appendix D — Airfoil Data

**Source:** Raymer, *Aircraft Design: A Conceptual Approach*, 6th ed. (AIAA, 2018), Appendix D
"Airfoil Data," printed pp. 971–986.

Coordinate tables (station and ordinate, in percent chord unless noted) for eight airfoils, each
followed by a multi-panel "Aerodynamic Characteristics" figure (lift, drag, and moment vs. angle
of attack / lift coefficient at several Reynolds numbers, plus a pitching-moment/a.c.-location
inset). The coordinate tables are reproduced faithfully below. The aerodynamic-characteristics
figures are wind-tunnel strip-chart-style plots with a fine embedded grid; their OCR text layer is
almost entirely non-numeric noise (axis tick labels are legible but the plotted curve traces are
not machine-readable), so they are catalogued but **not digitized** — see the note after each
airfoil's table.

**Book misprint, upper-surface station between 34.825 and 45.019 (checked 2026-08-18 against a
300-dpi render of book p. 977).** The page itself prints **"30.916"**; this is an error in the book,
not an OCR slip. The correct value is **39.916**. NACA 6-series upper and lower stations of this

also break the monotonic station sequence. Every other cell of both surfaces matches the page.
