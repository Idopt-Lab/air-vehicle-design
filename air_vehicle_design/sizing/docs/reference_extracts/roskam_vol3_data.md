# Roskam — *Airplane Design, Part III: Layout Design of Cockpit, Fuselage, Wing and Empennage (Cutaways and Inboard Profiles)* — Extracted Data

**Source:** Jan Roskam, *Airplane Design, Part III*, Roskam Aviation & Engineering Corp. (DARcorporation).
**Status:** Light pass only. This volume is a **scanned, drawing-heavy layout/structural-design** reference (cutaways and inboard profiles of existing aircraft). It contains **little of the analysis math** the MDA package needs — sizing/aero/weights/propulsion equations live in Parts I & II (see `roskam_vol1_data.md`, `roskam_vol2_data.md`).

> Citation: *Roskam, Airplane Design Part III, §section*.

## Contents (from OCR'd table of contents)
- **Ch. 2–3** Cockpit & fuselage layout design (interior arrangement, visibility, dimensions).
- **Ch. 4 Wing Layout Design** — structural design considerations: spar/rib/stiffener arrangements (§4.2.1), wing/fuselage integration (§4.2.3), wing cross-section (§4.2.4), lateral-control & high-lift device layout (§4.2.5–4.2.6), skin gages, maintenance/access; **§4.3 Military design considerations** (p.239).
- **Ch. 5 Empennage Layout Design** — configuration choice (conventional/canard) for stability & control (§5.1.3), stall/spin (§5.1.4), planform & airfoil selection (§5.1.5–5.1.6), **§5.1.7 Review of Empennage Drag Contributions** (p.273), structural integration.
- **Ch. 6 Integration of the Propulsion System** — engine/propeller data presentation; turbojet/turbofan integration (§6.1.4, p.301), inlet/duct layout.
