  {\textbf{One solver, two states --- and one coupling, not a second solver.}\\[3pt]
   The loop is a single fixed-point solver. It carries exactly two STATES,
   $W_{TO}$ and $T_{SL}$ (under-relaxed, tested to $10^{-6}$). Their feedback
   returns to the \emph{loop} --- the two solid red edges:\\[3pt]
   $\bullet$~\textbf{Closure $\rightarrow$ loop}: the new $W_{TO}$, as at Level~1.\\
   $\bullet$~\textbf{Constraint $\rightarrow$ loop}: $(T/W)^*$ sets the thrust,
   $T_{SL}=(T/W)^*\,W_{TO}$.\\[3pt]
   The \textbf{dashed amber} edge returns to a \emph{component}, not the loop. It
   is a coupling the \emph{same} solver closes by substitution --- not a monitored
   state, not a second solver:\\[3pt]
   $\bullet$~\textbf{Constraint $\rightarrow$ geometry}: $(W/S)^*$ resizes the wing
   next pass. It exists only because the Level-2 aero holds the geometry, so
   $C_{D0}$ moves as the wing shrinks ($C_{D0}\ 0.018\!\rightarrow\!0.022$,
   $S_{ref}\ 234\!\rightarrow\!175$~ft$^2$).\\[3pt]
   \textbf{Not feedback at all:} tail areas ($S_{ht},S_{vt}$) and the nacelle
   ($T_{SL}\!\rightarrow\!D_{inlet}\!\rightarrow\!C_{D0}$) flow \emph{forward}; both run
   once per pass, and nothing earlier reads them.};
