  {\textbf{Count the red edges. That is the whole difference.}\\[3pt]
   Level~1 had one feedback edge: the weight closure. Level~2 has five, and
   every extra one is a coupling Level~1 assumed away.\\[3pt]
   $\bullet$~\textbf{Tail $\rightarrow$ geometry.} The tail areas are written back, and the
   empty weight reads them.\\
   $\bullet$~\textbf{Propulsion $\rightarrow$ geometry.} Thrust sizes the nacelle, the nacelle
   changes the wetted area, the wetted area changes $C_{D0}$.\\
   $\bullet$~\textbf{Constraint $\rightarrow$ geometry and constraint $\rightarrow$ loop.} The design
   point is now an unknown too, re-solved on every pass.\\[3pt]
   Note also that the constraint block has moved \emph{inside} the repeat
   bracket, and that the aerodynamics block now has geometry feeding into it.
   Those two facts are the same fact.};
